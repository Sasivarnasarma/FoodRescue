# 13 — Decisions and Remaining Questions

The [MVP decision record](15_MVP_DECISIONS_AND_FREE_TIER.md) defines the finalized donation-only university scope. Verified organizations are the first recipient group. Whole-listing allocation, a simple approval screen and deterministic matching keep the implementation small.

Confirm the actual Firestore region, Firebase client registration, Expo starter and Android device setup during implementation. Check the assignment rubric for any mandatory AI demonstration. If required, scope the extra feature to a category suggestion that the donor confirms. An image cannot determine food condition or safety.

Before public launch, agree on real verification evidence, location and photo privacy, retention, category and allergen vocabulary, and support procedures. Use synthetic data for the university demo.
