# 07 — UI and UX Guidelines

| Role | Screens |
|---|---|
| Donor | Sign in, verification status, my listings, create/edit draft, incoming requests and match explanations, QR scanner, collection history |
| Organization | Sign in, verification status, nearby donations, listing details and request form, my requests, pickup directions and QR |
| Admin | Sign in, verification queue, profile review and audit history |

On listing cards show the food title, quantity and unit, distance when known, and pickup deadline. Use bundled images or placeholders for feeds and load the uploaded photo on detail. Label sample photos. In draft editing, preview the compressed image and show save progress, retry and fallback options. Use the device time zone to display the pickup window.

Request cards show the proposed pickup time, distance and declared storage compatibility. Explain why a request ranks higher. Approval assigns the whole donation. Confirm approval, cancellation and collection before committing. Status labels should distinguish open, assigned, collected, cancelled and expired.

Show loading, empty, retryable error, offline/cached, permission-denied and success states. Explain GPS and camera requests in context. Offer manual pickup location or reference entry where applicable. A scanned QR opens the record and indicates whether recipient confirmation is ready. Use labelled controls, readable contrast and accessible touch targets.
